# AI Use Disclosure - Template Only

**Important:** The Wharton Global Youth Program's current policy says AI-generated work may not be submitted as a team's own work. It also says AI use must be recorded in the Works Cited pages and AI-generated material retained in the submission must be cited. See the official policy: https://globalyouth.wharton.upenn.edu/ai-policy/ and the Investment Competition Rules & Roles page: https://globalyouth.wharton.upenn.edu/competitions/investment-competition/rules-roles/.

Do not copy this template unchanged. Edit it so that it accurately records what your team actually used, what material it retained, what your members independently verified, and what each member contributed. Do not claim independent verification unless it occurred.

## Suggested disclosure structure

- **Tool:** ChatGPT by OpenAI (include the model/version shown in your account, if requested by the competition).
- **Dates used:** [enter actual dates].
- **Purposes:** [describe accurately; examples may include brainstorming strategy structure, explaining financial concepts, drafting code for a historical-return bootstrap, checking arithmetic, formatting charts, or editing language].
- **Material retained:** [identify any AI-generated wording, code, explanations, charts, or analysis that remains in the submission or repository].
- **Team review:** [describe which team members independently checked calculations, traced data to original sources, ran tests, and edited or replaced AI-generated material].
- **Citation:** Include the AI tool and relevant details in the Works Cited section in the format required by the competition.

The team should ask its advisor or the competition organizers how to disclose AI use in the IPS if the IPS's no-footnotes/no-formal-citations formatting rules create an apparent conflict with the general AI policy.
