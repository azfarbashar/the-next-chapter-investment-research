# Final Report Chart Guide — The Next Chapter

Use four core charts in the official Final Report; a fifth is optional. The separate research appendix can retain diagnostic material, but the main report should not overwhelm judges.

## Four core charts

1. **Client cash-flow timeline (2027-2042).** Show the $300,000 beginning-2027 contribution, $150,000 beginning-2028 contribution, the beginning-2033 reserve establishment, and ten beginning-of-year $50,000 operating payments. Message: operating payments precede facility funding.
2. **Glide path and fallback.** Show five-point baseline targets of 65%, 60%, 55%, 50%, 45%, and 40% equity from 2027-2032, plus the eight-point fallback. Label them as policy targets, not forecasts.
3. **Historical/bootstrapped funding outcomes.** Show 5th percentile and median outcomes for the five-point and eight-point policies against both the illustrative 3% priced reserve and the $500,000 no-yield benchmark. Explain that the long history is U.S. stock/Treasury proxy data and that bootstrap path shares are model proportions, not real-world probabilities.
4. **Conditional 2031 facility contribution.** Show the $0 cap for lower 2031 balance bands and the conditional $20,000 central cap under the $500,000 reserve benchmark. State that the $20,000 upper end is not promised; recompute from actual 2031 balance and then-current 2033 zero curve.

## Optional chart

5. **Stock-Treasury correlation by regime.** Use only if the report explains that correlations change across economic periods and that Treasuries are not always negatively correlated with equities.

## Keep in the appendix or GitHub

- Full table of 93 overlapping historical six-year windows.
- Full Monte Carlo percentiles and reserve-rate sensitivities.
- Ten-rung reserve schedule and 2033-2042 roll-forward.
- Current StockTrak bond principal maturity bridge.
- 2%, 3%, and 5% inflation sensitivity.
- Full correlation matrix and selected historical stress years.

## Presentation rules

- Every chart needs one sentence stating its decision relevance, a labeled time period, and units.
- Define technical terms once in plain language.
- Distinguish the case-study long-term account from the StockTrak competition account.
- Do not label bootstrap path shares as real-world probabilities or guarantees.
- The IPS itself must remain text-only: the supplied IPS instructions prohibit graphics, charts, external links, footnotes, and formal citations.
